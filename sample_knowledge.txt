1. DATABASE SCHEMA CONTEXT
---------------------------
When queried about our financials, sales, or products, refer to the database schema tables. 
- "monthly_financials" contains our high-level revenue and margins.
- "product_metrics" breaks down our sales by fabric category.

2. BUSINESS CONTEXT & KEY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------
- REVENUE: All revenue is recorded in Indian Rupees (INR) Lakhs. 1 Lakh = 100,000 INR.
- COGS: Cost of Goods Sold includes raw cotton, spinning energy costs, and direct mill labor.
- GROSS MARGIN: Calculated as (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue. Our target gross margin is 25%.
- CATEGORIES: We specialize in "Premium Cotton", "Recycled Polyester", and "Jacquard Silk".

3. STRATEGIC INITIATIVES (2026)
--------------------------------
- Sustainability: We aim to increase Recycled Polyester production by 40% to meet European export demands.
- Energy Efficiency: We recently installed solar arrays at the Punjab facility, which is expected to reduce COGS by 3% in Q4.
- Supply Chain: Raw cotton prices are currently highly volatile due to monsoon delays.
